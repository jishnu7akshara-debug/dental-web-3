What happensat your first visit

About an hour from the moment you walk in. You can stop at any step and take the plan home.

  1. 1

    A conversation first

    Tell us what is bothering you, past experiences and anything that makes you nervous. We note it all in your file.

    10 minutes
  2. 2

    Scan and exam

    A 3D intraoral scan replaces messy moulds. Low dose digital X-rays only if we need them.

    20 minutes
  3. 3

    See it on screen

    We show you the scan, explain what we see in plain words and lay out every option, including doing nothing.

    15 minutes
  4. 4

    A written plan and fee

    You leave with a printed plan and exact costs. Start the same day or whenever you are ready.

    Yours to keep
